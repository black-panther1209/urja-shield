# UrjaShield

### AI-Powered Energy Supply Chain Resilience Platform

UrjaShield is an AI-powered decision-support platform designed to monitor geopolitical and logistics risks, simulate energy supply disruptions, and generate optimized procurement and routing recommendations.

## Problem Statement

**OOSC 4.0 Hackathon — Problem Statement 1**

**AI-Driven Energy Supply Chain Resilience for Import-Dependent Economies**

## Project Status

🚧 **Under Development**

## Planned Core Capabilities

* Geopolitical and logistics risk intelligence
* Energy supply disruption simulation
* Supply-gap and impact analysis
* Alternative procurement optimization
* Route optimization and recommendations
* Strategic reserve analysis
* Interactive energy resilience dashboard
